# Source and rights ledger

This app contains distilled planning ingredients only. It does not bundle the supplied PDFs, Markdown files, lyrics, screenshots or third-party images.

The future app licence will cover the app code and material Luke has the right to license. It will not automatically relicense third-party works, quoted research, credited diagrams or source documents. Creator, date or version, ownership and reuse permission marked `NEEDS CHECKING` needs resolution before public release.

## Short names used in supplied titles

Some source titles use specialised short names. The titles stay unchanged so the original files remain easy to identify.

- **AI** means artificial intelligence.
- **GAJRA** means Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth.
- **GGM** means Global Group Marriages.
- **NSI** means North Stradbroke Island. The story also uses the name Minjerribah with appropriate context.
- **RAG** means retrieval-augmented generation: finding relevant passages in a chosen collection before preparing an answer.
- **UAP** means unidentified aerial phenomena.
- **AA** means ancient aeronautics in the supplied Cosmic Nexus title.
- **R&D** means research and development.
- **C-Hour** means Community-Hour, a proposed way to recognise contributions to the commons.
