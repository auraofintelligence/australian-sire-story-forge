An open planning desk and book coach for an adult, travel-led universe mixing science fiction, romance, fantasy and hopeful near-future invention. Free to use, free to fork, free to point at your own place and your own people. It gives data, asks questions and places Luke's answers into patterns. It does not decide what happens in the fiction.

## Public page

[Open the published Australian Sire Story Forge](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-sire-story-forge/).

The app lets an author combine any number of story types, relationships, outside problems, story shapes and favourite ingredients from handwritten notes. It can plan novels, novellas, short stories, films, albums and interactive stories that follow, overlap, intersect, echo or hand consequences to one another.

The main sequence is Story Choices, Characters and Book Plan. Acts or parts, chapters, scenes and export sit inside the Book Plan. It carries sourced total-length bands and an editable calculator for total words, optional act or part shares, chapter count, scenes per chapter, total scenes, words per chapter, words per scene and estimated pages. Act, chapter and scene sizes are working calculations rather than claimed publishing rules. Connected Stories, the Idea Workshop and Story Patterns are optional workspaces outside the main sequence.

Tiggy Bestmann and Australian Sire are one main character. Tiggy begins as an aloof, playful systems dreamer who fears he is bluffing. Australian Sire is the name he earns through real wins, kept promises and adult trust. As Sire, he travels as a consensually chosen breeding male among adults who know he will not stay and choose the possibility of conception anyway.

Travel is a major force across the universe. Crystal City of Quandamooka Country is a recurring subterranean home, return point and long horizon, rather than the centre of every story. The real-world city remains a proposal with cultural authority, community desire, ecology, law and site control open. Its fictional expression stays visibly separate.

The Connected Stories page maps individual stories and their connections. The Idea Workshop turns optional writing worksheets into ways to explore clues, twists, knowledge, dialogue, pressure, relationships, consistent details, appearance, endings and hidden worlds. A loose note can be copied into another version, grouped with related ideas, linked to any story and any number of characters, or chosen for now by Luke. The Characters page helps build voice, motivation, everyday life, background, relationships, ceremony and details that should stay consistent. Its Aura LifeLogging questions preserve the supplied workbook as separate spatial idea groups rather than reading it as one flat table.

The handwritten worksheets are the starting field, not background decoration. Choose as many ideas as you like. “Use once” gives an idea one deliberate home, “Bring it back” repeats it wherever it fits, and “Shape the whole story” carries it through every suitable chapter. Each idea can connect to any number of later choices, including story type, Tiggy, relationships, situations, outside problems, story shape, intimacy, the ending, other characters, chapters, language and images. Those connections bring the idea forward without choosing anything automatically. A separate main-use choice helps place it in the chapter plan. The 2019 worksheets are dated possibilities. Each can be kept, ignored or privately rewritten to match what Luke means now. Unclear handwriting stays in a tray for later checking. Nothing on the shelf becomes a story law. The Connected Stories page lets Luke save a combination when a pattern emerges, remove it later or copy it into another story.

Fantasy-facing material calls the system Aura O.Z., expanded as Aura Operating Zeitgeist. Historical source titles remain unchanged in the source ledger. Relationship language throughout the live planner is polyamory and polyamorous erotica.

The wild possibility shelf also holds a network of subterranean, undersea, myth-inspired and wholly invented civilisations. Independent realms can send adult emissaries towards emerging leaders before technological change becomes unpredictable or civilisation faces a survival hurdle. These are fictional possibilities, not claims that distinct traditions share one origin. Each can be selected, combined, contradicted, rewritten or ignored.

## Open it

Double-click the **Start Story Forge** desktop icon. You can also double-click `START STORY FORGE.cmd` inside this folder. The local preview starts quietly and opens in your browser.

Your projects are stored in your own browser. No account, no cloud, nobody else can see them. Use **Download editable backup** from the app before clearing browser data or changing computers. Updating a chapter plan also keeps up to three recoverable earlier versions inside the project.
