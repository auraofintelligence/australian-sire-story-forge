Book Plan 2 of 5

Optional large sections

Does this story want acts or parts?

Acts and parts are large sections containing chapters. Choose a starting split, rename every section, change its relative size or leave the book as one continuous chapter plan.

Relative size compares the sections with one another. For example, 25, 50, 25 makes the middle section about twice as large. The numbers do not need to total 100.
