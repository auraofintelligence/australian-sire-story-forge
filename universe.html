Many journeys.
One living possibility space.

Gather possibilities, play with them until patterns emerge, then save the ones that may become novels, short stories, films, albums or interactive stories. Stories can follow, overlap, meet, echo or hand consequences to one another. Travel is the large engine. Crystal City is one recurring anchor and horizon among many. Hidden civilisations and their adult emissaries can emerge differently across separate journeys, with no single story owning the complete explanation.
